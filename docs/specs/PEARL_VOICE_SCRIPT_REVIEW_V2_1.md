## Session Entry and Universal Safety

1. Time to train. I will guide the setup, work, and rests. Follow my voice and pause whenever you need.
2. Clear the space around you. Stop for sharp pain, dizziness, or feeling unwell.
Complete duration: Clara 12610 ms at 250 ms gaps.

## Equipment First-Use Lines

- Use a sturdy chair that will not slide.
- Keep support within easy reach.
- Use the lowest stable step, with support nearby.
- Check the band first and keep it away from your face.
- Use a secure closed door anchor and test light tension first.
- Move down to the floor and settle into the start position.

## Shared Setup and Final-Position Readiness

- Step into view so your whole body is visible.
- I need a clearer view. Make sure your whole body is visible.
- Move to the centre of the view.
- Move a little farther back.
- Move a little closer.
- Hold still for a moment.
- Please turn on the main light.
- You're set.
