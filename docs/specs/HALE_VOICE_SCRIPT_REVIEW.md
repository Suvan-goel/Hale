# Hale Voice Script Review

## Review Principles

Review for calm, precise, adult language. Each line should describe exactly what the user should do, fit the timing budget, sound natural in both voices, and avoid medical claims, form critique, implementation language, and unnecessary praise.

## Training Session

### Session entry

1. Time to train. I will guide each setup, set, and rest. Follow my voice; the screen is here for backup.
2. Before we start, clear the space around you. Stop for sharp pain, dizziness, or feeling unwell. Keep supports and equipment steady.

### Universal safety

Before we start, clear the space around you. Stop for sharp pain, dizziness, or feeling unwell. Keep supports and equipment steady.

### Equipment first-use checks

- chair: Use a sturdy chair that will not slide or tip.
- support: Keep sturdy support within easy reach.
- step_or_stair: Use the lowest stable step, with support nearby, and keep the phone out of your stepping path.
- long_band: Check the band first. Keep it away from your face and return it slowly.
- door_anchor: Use a purpose-built anchor on a fully closed door, and test light tension before the set.
- mini_band: Place the mini band securely and use small controlled steps.
- floor_transfer: Use clear floor space and move slowly getting down and back up. Keep support nearby.
- balance: Keep fingertips near support and touch it whenever you need.

## Movement Check-Up

### Session entry

Welcome to your Movement Check-Up. I will guide four short movements for strength, balance, and mobility. Follow my voice.

### Chair stand

1. First is the thirty second chair stand. Sit side-on, in the middle of a sturdy chair, feet flat.
2. Cross your arms over your chest. Stand fully and sit back down as many times as you can until I say time.
3. Three. Two. One. Go!
4. Time.
5. You completed [number] chair stand/chair stands.
6. Zero-rep line: I could not measure a completed stand that time. You can retry or continue.

### Balance ladder

- Next are balance holds. Face the phone with fingertips near steady support.
- I will cue each foot position. Hold until the next cue, or touch support if you need.
- Feet together, side by side. Hold steady.
- Keep holding, and gently close your eyes.
- You can open your eyes now.
- Left foot in front, heel to toe. Hold steady.
- Keep holding, and gently close your eyes.
- You can open your eyes now.
- Stand on your left leg. Lift the right foot just off the floor.

### Shoulder reach

- Next is shoulder reach. Turn side-on and stand tall, left arm relaxed at your side.
- When I say go, raise your left arm straight forward and up as high as comfortable, then hold.
- Lower your arm and relax.
- Nicely done.

### Forward reach

- Next is forward reach. Stay side-on, feet under hips, standing tall.
- When I say go, fold forward from your hips and reach both hands toward the floor, then hold.
- Slowly stand tall.
- Nicely done.

### Completion

That is the whole check-up. Your results are ready on the screen.
