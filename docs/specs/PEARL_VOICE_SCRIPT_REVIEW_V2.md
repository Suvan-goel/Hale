# Pearl Voice Script Review V2

## How to Review

Read each spoken line aloud. Lines marked `[DECISION REQUIRED: FD-xxx]` are proposed language only and are not frozen. This document intentionally omits source-code tables.

## Session-Wide Training Lines

- training-intro-v2: Time to train. I'll guide each setup, set, and rest. Follow my voice and pause whenever you need.
- safe-session-start-v2: Before we start, clear the space around you. Stop for sharp pain, dizziness, or feeling unwell. Keep support steady.
- completion-v2: Session complete. Well done.
## Equipment First-Use Lines

- safe-chair-first-v2: Use a sturdy chair that will not slide or tip.
- safe-support-first-v2: Keep sturdy support within easy reach.
- safe-step-first-v2: Use the lowest stable step, keep support nearby, and keep the phone out of your stepping path.
- safe-band-first-v2: Check the band first. Keep it away from your face and return it slowly.
- safe-door-anchor-first-v2: Use a purpose-built anchor on a fully closed door. Test light tension before the set.
- safe-mini-band-first-v2 [DECISION REQUIRED: FD-004]: Place the mini band securely above your knees and use small controlled steps.
- safe-floor-first-v2: Use clear floor space and move slowly getting down and back up.
- safe-balance-first-v2: Keep fingertips near support and touch it whenever you need.

- close-your-eyes-v2 [DECISION REQUIRED: FD-003]: If approved for this check, gently close your eyes.
- shoulder-intro-v2 [DECISION REQUIRED: FD-002]: Turn so the arm being measured faces the phone. Stand tall with that arm relaxed.
- shoulder-setup-v2 [DECISION REQUIRED: FD-002]: On go, raise that arm forward and up as high as comfortable, then hold.
- microcheck-balance-v2 [DECISION REQUIRED: FD-002]: Quick balance check. Fingertips near support, stand on your selected leg, and hold as long as comfortable.
- microcheck-mobility-v2 [DECISION REQUIRED: FD-002]: Quick mobility check. Sit tall at the chair edge, one leg straight, and reach gently toward your toes.
