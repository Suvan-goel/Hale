# Pearl documentation authority

Pearl's current product definition lives in, in order:

1. `AGENTS.md` — binding audience, product laws, MVP scope, architecture, and check-up protocol.
2. `README.md` — current repository and mounted-product overview.
3. `Idea.md` — current product and business thesis.
4. The latest applicable entry in `docs/decisions.md` — dated decisions and rationale.

The current Pearl MVP is a private 12-week strength programme for women roughly 45–60 in
perimenopause or early postmenopause. Four comparable check-ups measure Strength and Balance;
optional Everyday Clarity self-report is tracked separately. Daily training is voice-paced and
camera-free.

## Historical material

`docs/audits/`, dated remediation reports, migration plans, old QA protocols, and handoffs are
retained as engineering evidence. Many describe retired shells, full movement batteries,
Mobility scoring, age-style scores, or experimental Clarity instruments. Their dates and
findings may still matter when tracing code, but they do not define Pearl's current audience,
positioning, information architecture, or shipped MVP.

When any historical document conflicts with `AGENTS.md`, `AGENTS.md` wins.
